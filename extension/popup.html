✦ Prompt Coach

Rewrites your prompt before you send it. Bring your own API key — it's stored locally in your browser and never sent anywhere except your chosen provider.

Run ollama serve and ollama pull llama3.1 first. Origin/CORS is handled automatically — no extra setup.

When on, Coach reads the last few messages on the page to understand intent (e.g. resolve "like before"). History is reference-only — never copied into your prompt. Adds tokens to the Coach call.

On ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, type a prompt and click the floating ✦ Coach button.
